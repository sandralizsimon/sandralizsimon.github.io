About

I am a doctoral researcher in the Computational Catalysis and Interfacial Chemistry Laboratory at IIT Kanpur, advised by Prof. Vishal Agarwal and Prof. Nitin Kaistha. I joined the Direct-PhD program in 2021 after graduating as gold medalist in Chemical Engineering from NIT Calicut.

My work merges computational chemistry and machine learning. Locating the transition state of a reaction on a DFT potential energy surface can take hundreds of expensive force evaluations. I develop Gaussian-process-based active-learning methods that learn the surface as the reaction path evolves and spend DFT calls only at the most informative locations on the potential energy surface. The goal is faster, cheaper screening of catalysts for industrially important reactions.

Research

Reaction path discovery on potential energy surfaces

The minimum energy path is the lowest-energy route between two stable states, and its highest point, the saddle point, sets the reaction rate in harmonic transition state theory. Methods like the nudged elastic band find this path reliably, but each iteration needs DFT forces at many images. My methods cut that cost so harder, larger reactions become practical to study.

AL-NEB

JCTC 2026

An active-learning framework that couples joint energy–gradient Gaussian process regression with the nudged elastic band for automated transition-state discovery. Cuts DFT force evaluations by more than an order of magnitude at the same accuracy, validated on gas-phase reactions and surface diffusion with up to 525 degrees of freedom.

GPyTorch · ASE · VASP · Gaussian16 · MATLAB

AARE and Acc-CG

React. Chem. Eng. 2025

Two optimizers for structures and minimum energy paths: AARE (Adaptively Accelerated Relaxation Engine), a molecular-dynamics-based optimizer with adaptive acceleration, and Acc-CG, an accelerated conjugate gradient method. Both work as drop-in optimizers in ASE.

ASE · Python

Multi-fidelity active learning

In preparation

Combines cheap low-fidelity and expensive high-fidelity simulation data through co-kriging to locate transition states at high-fidelity accuracy with far fewer DFT evaluations. Manuscript and software release in preparation.

Co-kriging · ASE · VASP · Gaussian16

Moving-Anchor AL-NEB

In preparation

Robust transition-state identification and lower-cost path exploration, validated on the Baker test set and on CH₄ decomposition over ZnCl₂, H₂ dissociation on Au(111), keto–enol tautomerization in a zeolite cluster, and methane pyrolysis over molten sodium.
